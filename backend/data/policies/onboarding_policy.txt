ONBOARDING POLICY — Acme Corp

1. FIRST WEEK REQUIREMENTS
Every new employee must complete the following within their first week:
- HR documentation: offer letter acceptance, ID proof submission, bank details, and emergency contacts.
- Mandatory security and compliance training (information security, data privacy, code of conduct).
- IT asset collection: laptop, access card, and device configuration with IT support.
- Introduction meetings: manager 1:1, team introduction, and mentor/buddy kickoff.

2. ONBOARDING BUDDY
- Each new hire is assigned a mentor (onboarding buddy) from their team.
- The mentor meets the new hire at least twice a week during the first month.

3. FIRST 30 DAYS
- Complete role-specific setup (development environment, tools access, repository permissions).
- Read the department handbook (engineering handbook, design guidelines, or HR manual as applicable).
- Ship a first small deliverable (for engineering: a starter ticket through code review).

4. ONBOARDING REVIEWS
- A 30-day check-in is conducted by the manager.
- A 90-day confirmation review is conducted before probation ends.

5. PROBATION
- The standard probation period is 3 months, extendable at the manager's discretion.
- Onboarding tasks must be completed before the probation review.
