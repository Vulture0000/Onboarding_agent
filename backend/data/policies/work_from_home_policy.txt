WORK FROM HOME POLICY — Acme Corp

1. ELIGIBILITY
- Employees who have completed 30 days of onboarding are eligible for hybrid work.
- New hires during probation are expected to work from the office at least 4 days per week to support onboarding.

2. HYBRID SCHEDULE
- Standard arrangement: 3 days in office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are anchor days) and 2 days remote.
- Team-level variations require manager approval.

3. FULLY REMOTE WORK
- Extended fully-remote work (more than 2 consecutive weeks) requires HR and manager approval.
- Employees working remotely from a different city must inform HR for tax and compliance reasons.

4. REMOTE WORK EXPECTATIONS
- Employees must be reachable during core hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
- A stable internet connection and a quiet workspace are required for meetings.
- Attendance must be marked on the HR portal on remote days.

5. EQUIPMENT AND SUPPORT
- The company provides a laptop and optional monitor/headset allowance for remote work.
- IT support is available remotely through the helpdesk portal.

6. WORK FROM HOME ON MEDICAL GROUNDS
- Employees recovering from illness may request temporary work-from-home with manager approval, without using sick leave.
