ATTENDANCE POLICY — Acme Corp

1. WORKING HOURS
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- Total working time: 40 hours per week.
- Employees are expected to maintain core hours between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM for meetings and collaboration.

2. FLEXIBLE TIMINGS
- Start time may be flexed between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM with manager consent, provided core hours are covered.

3. LATE ARRIVAL
- Three late arrivals (after 10:30 AM) in a month count as one half-day leave.

4. ATTENDANCE RECORDING
- Attendance is recorded via the office access card or the HR portal for remote employees.
- Missed punches must be regularized within 3 working days through the HR portal.

5. OVERTIME & COMP-OFF
- Work on weekends or public holidays is compensated with comp-off days, subject to manager approval.
- Comp-off must be availed within 60 days of earning it.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- The company observes 10 public holidays per year, announced in the holiday calendar each January.
