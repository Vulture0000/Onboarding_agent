LEAVE POLICY — Acme Corp (Effective January 2026)

1. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS (per calendar year)
- Casual Leave (CL): 8 days. Intended for short personal matters. Cannot be carried forward or encashed.
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days. Medical certificate required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.
- Earned Leave (EL): 12 days. Accrued at 1 day per completed month of service. Can be carried forward up to 30 days.
- New joiners receive a pro-rated leave allocation based on their joining date.

2. APPROVAL RULES
- Casual or sick leave of up to 2 consecutive days: auto-approved if leave balance is sufficient; manager is notified.
- Any leave request longer than 2 consecutive days requires manager approval.
- Earned leave always requires manager approval regardless of duration.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least 3 working days in advance, except in emergencies.
- Sick leave may be applied retrospectively within 2 working days of returning.

3. LEAVE BALANCE
- Leave balance is checked automatically at the time of request. Requests exceeding the available balance are rejected.
- Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December each year.

4. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
- If all paid leave balances are exhausted, leave without pay may be granted with HR and manager approval.

5. RESTRICTIONS
- A maximum of 5 consecutive leave days of any combination requires prior approval.
- Employees on probation (first 3 months) may use casual and sick leave only.
- Leave during the notice period is restricted to the available earned leave balance.
